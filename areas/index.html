Areas we cover in Stoke-on-Trent and nearby

Stoke-on-Trent is six towns rather than one, and each has its own mix of housing and tenants. We let and manage property across all six and in Newcastle-under-Lyme.

Stoke-on-Trent overview

The city as a whole.

Hanley

The city centre, including Shelton, Etruria, Bucknall, Bentilee and Abbey Hulton nearby.

Burslem

The Mother Town, with a large stock of terraced housing.

Tunstall

The most northerly town, including nearby Chell.

Stoke

Stoke-upon-Trent, around the railway station, including Trent Vale.

Fenton

Between Stoke and Longton.

Longton

The southern town, including Meir and Blurton.

Newcastle-under-Lyme

The neighbouring market town, including Kidsgrove.

We also work with landlords in Stone, Leek, Endon, Alsager and other places around the city. If your property is nearby, ask us.
