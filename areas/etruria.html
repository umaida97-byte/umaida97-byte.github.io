Area guide

Etruria Property Market Guide

Property types

Etruria is best known as the site of Josiah Wedgwood's original factory and Etruria Hall, and as the junction of the Trent and Mersey and Caldon canals. Today it combines industrial heritage — including the Etruria Industrial Museum — with newer apartment development close to Festival Park and the city centre.

Rental & tenant demand

Demand comes from tenants wanting city-centre proximity in newer apartment stock, alongside interest tied to Festival Park's retail, leisure and hotel development immediately adjacent.

Investment characteristics

The newer apartment stock in Etruria suits investors targeting young professional tenants close to Hanley, with a different profile to the Victorian terraced streets found across most of the six towns.

Transport & nearby areas

Etruria railway station and Festival Park sit within walking distance of Hanley city centre. Neighbours Hanley and Shelton.
