Leek Property Market Guide

Property types

Known as the 'Queen of the Moorlands', Leek is a historic market town with a silk and textile heritage, an attractive town centre, and easy access to the Peak District National Park - giving it a distinctly different character to Stoke-on-Trent's six towns.

Rental & tenant demand

Demand comes from those wanting market-town and countryside living within reach of Stoke-on-Trent, Macclesfield and the Peak District, with a broad mix of families, commuters and those drawn to the town's outdoor-access appeal.

Investment characteristics

Leek's tourism and countryside appeal alongside its market-town amenities make it attractive for investors focused on capital growth and a stable tenant base, generally at a higher price point than the six towns.

Transport & nearby areas

The A53 connects Leek to Stoke-on-Trent and the Peak District; the nearest mainline rail access is via Stoke-on-Trent station.
