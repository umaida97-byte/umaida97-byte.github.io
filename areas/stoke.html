Stoke (Stoke-upon-Trent) Property Market Guide

Property types

Stoke-upon-Trent gives the wider city its name and remains its administrative and civic heart, home to Stoke Minster, the main railway station on the West Coast Main Line, and Staffordshire University's main campus. Housing ranges from Victorian terraces to housing built around the university and hospital.

Rental & tenant demand

Demand here is more mixed than the other towns, with the university and Royal Stoke University Hospital supporting steady demand from students, staff and young professionals alongside established family housing.

Investment characteristics

Proximity to the mainline station and university gives Stoke a different tenant profile to the more purely residential towns - worth considering for investors targeting professional or academic tenants specifically.

Transport & nearby areas

Stoke-on-Trent railway station offers direct services to London, Manchester and Birmingham. Borders Trent Vale and Shelton.
