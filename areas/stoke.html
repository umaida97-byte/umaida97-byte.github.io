The Stoke rental market

  • Town centre, Boothen and Oakhill: Victorian terraces and steady, affordable lets
  • Penkhull: a hilltop village and conservation area with period cottages and larger homes, popular with professionals and families
  • Hartshill: Victorian villas, terraces and flats right next to the Royal Stoke University Hospital, one of the region's largest employers
  • Trent Vale and Hanford: interwar and post-war semis, popular with families and close to the A500 and junction 15 of the M6

Who rents here: NHS and hospital staff (including people on fixed-term contracts and rotations), staff and students at Staffordshire University, commuters using Stoke-on-Trent station, and local families.

Living in Stoke

Stoke has the city's mainline railway station, with direct trains to London Euston, Manchester and Birmingham. It is also home to the King's Hall and Civic Centre, Stoke Minster, and the old Spode Works, now being reused for creative businesses and events. The bet365 Stadium and Trentham Gardens are a few minutes south.
