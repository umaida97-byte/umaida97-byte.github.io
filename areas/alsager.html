Alsager Property Market Guide

Property types

Technically just across the border in Cheshire, Alsager is commonly considered as part of the wider Stoke-on-Trent commuter belt. It has a more affluent, market-town character than the six towns, with good schools and a settled residential feel.

Rental & tenant demand

Rental demand comes largely from families and commuters wanting a market-town setting with easy rail access, generally at a higher price point than Stoke-on-Trent proper.

Investment characteristics

Alsager suits investors targeting a higher-quality tenant profile and steadier, lower-turnover family lets, generally with a higher entry price and correspondingly lower gross yield than the six towns.

Transport & nearby areas

Alsager railway station connects to Manchester and Crewe. Sits just north of Stoke-on-Trent toward the Cheshire border.
