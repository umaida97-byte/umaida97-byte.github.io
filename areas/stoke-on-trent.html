Why a local letting agent matters in Stoke-on-Trent

Getting around

Stoke-on-Trent station is on the West Coast Main Line, with direct trains to London, Manchester and Birmingham. The A500 links the six towns from north to south, the A50 heads east to Derby, and the M6 runs along the western edge of the city.
