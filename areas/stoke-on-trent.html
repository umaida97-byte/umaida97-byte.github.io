City overview

Stoke-on-Trent Property Market Guide

Stoke-on-Trent is a federation of six towns - Hanley, Burslem, Tunstall, Longton, Fenton and Stoke - each with a distinct property market, plus the surrounding Staffordshire villages and market towns.

Property market

The city's housing stock is mixed: dense Victorian and Edwardian terraces close to the old pottery works, interwar semis further out, post-war estates, and a growing amount of new-build and city-centre apartment development, particularly around Hanley. Prices remain relatively affordable compared with many other UK cities, which shapes both the buyer and tenant market.

Who it suits

First-time buyers priced out of nearby cities, buy-to-let investors drawn by relative affordability, and a settled population of long-term local residents across the more residential towns. Rental demand is strongest around the city centre, the universities, and the main hospital sites.

Transport

Stoke-on-Trent railway station sits on the West Coast Main Line, with direct services toward London, Manchester and Birmingham. The M6 runs along the western edge of the city, and the A50/A500 connect the six towns to each other and to the wider road network.
