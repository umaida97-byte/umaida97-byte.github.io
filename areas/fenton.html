Fenton Property Market Guide

Property types

The smallest of the six towns and, notably, the one Arnold Bennett omitted from his fictional 'Five Towns' novels, Fenton sits between Longton and Stoke with a similar Victorian terraced character shaped by its own pottery heritage.

Rental & tenant demand

A quieter rental market than its larger neighbours, with demand centred on affordable family terraced housing and easy access to both Stoke and Longton town centres.

Investment characteristics

Fenton's smaller size means less choice of stock, but also less competition among investors - worth considering alongside neighbouring Longton for a similar buyer or tenant profile.

Transport & nearby areas

Sits between Stoke and Longton with good bus links to both; the A50 passes close by.
