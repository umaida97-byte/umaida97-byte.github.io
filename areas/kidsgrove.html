Kidsgrove Property Market Guide

Property types

Sitting on the city's northern edge toward the Cheshire border, Kidsgrove grew up around the Trent and Mersey Canal and the Harecastle Tunnel, and later around coal mining and engineering. It retains a mix of Victorian terraces, interwar housing and newer estate development.

Rental & tenant demand

A popular choice for commuter tenants thanks to Kidsgrove railway station on the West Coast Main Line, alongside family demand for its more suburban, semi-rural feel compared to the six towns.

Investment characteristics

The rail link makes Kidsgrove attractive to investors targeting commuter tenants working in Manchester, Stoke or further afield, and its newer estate housing can suit family lets with lower maintenance overhead than older terraced stock.

Transport & nearby areas

Kidsgrove railway station connects to Manchester and Stoke-on-Trent. Borders Tunstall and the Cheshire border toward Alsager.
