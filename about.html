About Five Towns

An independent, family-run property company

Five Towns has worked the Stoke-on-Trent property market for more than 20 years, across two generations of the same family. That's the whole pitch, really: we live here, we invest here, and we've been doing this long enough to know what actually works.

Our history

Five Towns began as a small, local letting operation and grew alongside the city's own regeneration — through the change in Hanley's city centre, the shift in tenant demand across the six towns, and the gradual arrival of a more established buy-to-let market in Stoke-on-Trent. The business has stayed independent throughout, and it remains run day-to-day by the same family that founded it.

Our philosophy

We think good property management is mostly about attention: referencing tenants properly, answering the phone, doing the inspection you said you'd do, and telling a landlord the truth about what a property will actually achieve rather than the number that wins the instruction. It's not complicated, but it does need doing consistently.

Local knowledge

Because our work spans lettings, sales, management and investment advice across the same handful of towns, we see the market from more angles than an agency that only sells or only lets. That cross-view is genuinely useful when a landlord is deciding whether to sell or re-let, or when an investor is weighing up two similar streets in different areas.

Why people choose Five Towns

Landlords

Because we manage property the way we'd want our own managed — and because we're landlords ourselves, we don't promise what we wouldn't believe.

Sellers

Because we value properties against what's genuinely selling locally, not against what gets us the instruction.

Investors

Because we know the difference a specific street makes to yield and demand, not just the city-wide averages.

Tenants & buyers

Because we respond, we're straightforward, and we don't disappear once the tenancy or sale is agreed.
