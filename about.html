How we work

We're landlords ourselves, so we manage every property the way we'd want ours managed. That means referencing tenants properly, answering the phone, doing the inspections we say we'll do, and giving landlords a realistic rent figure rather than the one that wins the instruction.

Much of our letting happens through the network of tenants and landlords we've built up over the years. When a property needs wider marketing, we advertise on portals such as Zoopla.

Because we also handle sales, maintenance, refurbishment and investment sourcing, we can give a straight answer when a landlord is deciding whether to sell, refurbish or re-let.
