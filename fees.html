Fees

Our Fees

Clear, published charges for our lettings and sales services, no hidden extras. All fees below are shown plus VAT.

Lettings Fees

Let Only

£450 + VAT

A one-off fee covering marketing, viewings, referencing and tenancy set-up. Once the tenancy begins, ongoing management is left in your hands.

See what's included

Full Management

10% + VAT

Charged monthly on the rent collected. Covers tenant find, rent collection, inspections, maintenance coordination and compliance tracking for the life of the tenancy.

See what's included

Maintenance and works: any repairs or maintenance work is always quoted in advance and requires your approval before we proceed. There's no charge for arranging or quoting the work itself.

Sales Fees

Selling your property

1% + VAT

Charged on the agreed sale price on completion, subject to a minimum fee of £1,000 + VAT. Covers valuation, marketing, viewings, negotiation and sales progression through to completion.

See how we sell your property

A note on VAT and estimates

All fees above are shown exclusive of VAT, which is charged at the standard rate in addition. Figures given anywhere else on this site (for example in our property investment guides) are illustrative examples only and are not fee quotes - the fees on this page are our actual current charges.

For anything not covered here, or to confirm how a fee applies to your specific property, get in touch and we'll give you a straight answer.
