Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains how five-towns.co.uk uses cookies, in line with the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be used to make a site work, to remember your preferences, or to track how you use the site.

Cookies this website uses

This website does not currently set any analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If that changes in future, for example if we add analytics to understand how the site is used, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required by law.

Third-party services

Our contact form is processed by a third-party provider, Web3Forms, in order to deliver enquiries to us. This may involve their own use of cookies or data processing, governed by their own privacy policy, which we'd encourage you to review if you have concerns.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies is unlikely to significantly affect your use of this website, since we don't currently rely on them for core functionality.

More information

For general guidance on cookies and your rights, see the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
