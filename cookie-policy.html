Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains how the {{ site.data.company.name }} website uses cookies, in line with UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can make a site work, remember your preferences, or track how you use it.

Cookies this website uses

This website does not currently set any cookies, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If we add analytics in future, we'll ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

Our enquiry forms are processed by Web3Forms, which delivers your message to our inbox. Only the details you enter in the form are sent. See our privacy policy for how we handle enquiry data.

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp or open our Google Business Profile, those services' own privacy and cookie policies apply.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

More information
