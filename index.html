Why Five Towns

Two generations in the same six towns.

Five Towns was built on a simple premise: that a property company working in Stoke-on-Trent should actually know Stoke-on-Trent - its streets, its tenant demand, its yields, and the difference a mile can make to a rental valuation. Over twenty years and two generations, that local knowledge has stayed the constant.

We are independent and family-run, which means the people you speak to are the people who make the decisions - not a call centre reading from a script. And because we are landlords ourselves, we manage every property the way we would want our own managed: properly referenced tenants, realistic valuations, and maintenance that gets sorted rather than logged.

For landlords

Your property. Our expertise.

Owning a rental property is straightforward. Running one properly - sourcing and referencing the right tenant, staying compliant, keeping on top of maintenance, collecting rent on time, every time - is a job in itself. Five Towns takes that job on, as much or as little of it as you want handed over.

For investors

Invest locally. Think long term.

Stoke-on-Trent is one of the more affordable city property markets in England relative to many other UK cities, which is part of why it attracts buy-to-let interest - but affordability on its own doesn't make an investment work. Rental demand, tenant type, regeneration activity, transport links and the specific street all matter more than the headline city-level story.

We work through those factors area by area rather than treating Stoke-on-Trent as a single market, and we're upfront when a figure is illustrative rather than a guaranteed return - see our property investment pages for area-by-area detail.

Explore Property Investment in Stoke-on-Trent
  • Relative affordability compared with many other UK city markets
  • Established rental demand across several town centres
  • Ongoing regeneration in and around Hanley and the wider city centre
  • Universities, hospitals and manufacturing employers supporting tenant demand
  • Road and rail links into Manchester, Birmingham and London
  • A range of strategies: single-lets, HMOs, and refurbishment-to-let

For sellers

When it's time to sell, local knowledge matters.

An accurate valuation and the right marketing bring the right buyers - not just the most viewings. We price properties against what is genuinely selling in that specific area of Stoke-on-Trent, market them properly, and stay on top of the sale through to completion.
