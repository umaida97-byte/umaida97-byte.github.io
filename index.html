How letting with us works

  1. Free rental valuationWe look at the property and tell you what it should let for, what it needs before letting, and which service suits you.
  2. Finding the right tenantWe match the property to applicants from our network first, then advertise if needed. Every applicant is referenced and right-to-rent checked.
  3. Move-inTenancy agreement signed, inventory and check-in completed, deposit registered with the Deposit Protection Service (DPS).
  4. Ongoing managementRent collected and paid to you with a statement, repairs arranged within your limit, inspections twice a year, compliance dates tracked.
