What we do

Property services across Stoke-on-Trent

From a single tenant-find instruction to full portfolio management, refurbishment, sales and investment sourcing — here's the detail on each service.

Tenant Find

We find and reference a suitable tenant, then hand the tenancy over to you fully set up.

Let Only

Marketing, viewings and referencing, with rent collection and management left in your hands.

Rent Collection

We find the tenant and collect the rent monthly, chasing arrears so you don't have to.

Property Management

Full day-to-day management of your rental property, start to finish.

Property Maintenance

Reactive and planned maintenance, coordinated by people who know the property.

Property Refurbishment

Refurbishment for letting or sale, from a light refresh to a full renovation.

Property Sourcing

We identify investment property that matches your strategy and budget.

Property Sales

Valuation, marketing and negotiation through to a completed sale.

Property Valuations

A free, evidenced valuation for letting or selling — no obligation.

Investment Advice

Independent guidance on strategy, area and yield for buy-to-let investors.
