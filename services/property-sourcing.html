Property sourcing for landlords and investors in Stoke-on-Trent

Whether you're adding to a portfolio or buying your first rental property, we find property in Stoke-on-Trent that suits your budget and strategy, and can let and manage it once you own it.

What's included

Understanding your strategy and budget, identifying suitable opportunities - on and off-market where possible - and assessing each against realistic rental income, condition and any refurbishment cost.

Our approach

We don't source deals to hit a target number; we source property we'd be comfortable recommending to a landlord we know personally, because in practice, that's often exactly who's asking.

Who this is for

Existing landlords adding to their portfolio, and investors buying their first rental property, who want a local team that also lets and manages property here. You get realistic rent figures from people who let homes in these streets, rather than numbers from a nationwide deal list.

Choose "Landlord" or "Property investor" in the form and include your budget, the type of property and the areas you're interested in. We'll call you to talk it through.
