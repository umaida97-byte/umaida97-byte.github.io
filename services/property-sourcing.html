What's included

Understanding your strategy and budget, identifying suitable opportunities - on and off-market where possible - and assessing each against realistic rental income, condition and any refurbishment cost.

Our approach

We don't source deals to hit a target number; we source property we'd be comfortable recommending to a landlord we know personally, because in practice, that's often exactly who's asking.

Who this is for

Investors buying their first property, or building a portfolio, who want local sourcing expertise rather than a nationwide deal list.
