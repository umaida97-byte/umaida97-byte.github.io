Rent Collection in Stoke-on-Trent

We find the tenant and collect the rent every month, chasing any arrears — while you look after maintenance and inspections yourself.

Get a Quote

What's included

Everything in our tenant-find service, plus monthly rent collection, prompt arrears follow-up, and monthly statements so you always know where things stand.

Why landlords choose this level

It removes the more awkward admin — chasing late payment — while keeping maintenance and inspection decisions in the landlord's hands, often because they use their own trusted contractors.

Who this is for

Landlords local to their property, or with the time to manage maintenance and inspections directly, who want rent collection taken off their plate.
