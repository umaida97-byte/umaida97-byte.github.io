Property Management in Stoke-on-Trent

Full, hands-off management of your rental property — tenant find, rent collection, maintenance, inspections and compliance, handled by a local team that answers the phone.

Request a Management QuoteSwitching Agent?

What's included

Full property management is our most complete service, built for landlords who want the property handled properly without being involved in the day-to-day. We act as the single point of contact for your tenant, and for you.

  • Tenant finding, referencing and right-to-rent checks
  • Tenancy agreement preparation and deposit protection
  • Monthly rent collection, with arrears followed up promptly
  • Routine property inspections, with written reports
  • Maintenance coordination, using vetted contractors
  • Compliance tracking: gas safety, EICR, EPC, smoke and CO alarms
  • Renewals, rent reviews and tenancy administration
  • End-of-tenancy check-out and deposit resolution

Who this is for

Landlords who live outside Stoke-on-Trent, own more than one property, don't have time to manage tenancies personally, or have been caught out by a previous agent's lack of communication. It also suits first-time landlords who want the reassurance of someone experienced handling compliance from day one.

Process

  1. Valuation & instruction — we assess the property and agree the management level.
  2. Marketing & referencing — professional photography, portal listing, viewings and tenant referencing.
  3. Move-in — agreement signed, deposit protected, inventory completed.
  4. Ongoing management — rent collection, inspections, maintenance and compliance for the life of the tenancy.

Frequently asked questions

What does a property management company actually do?

A property management company handles the day-to-day running of a rental property on the landlord's behalf: finding and referencing tenants, collecting rent, arranging maintenance, carrying out inspections, and keeping the tenancy compliant with current legislation.

How much does property management cost in Stoke-on-Trent?

Fees vary by agency and by the level of service included. We provide a specific quote once we understand the property and the level of management you need — get in touch for a management quote with no obligation.

Can I switch to Five Towns from my current letting agent?

Yes. We handle the transfer, including notifying your tenant, checking notice periods on your current agreement, and picking up ongoing compliance requirements such as gas safety and EICR renewal dates. See our guide to changing letting agent.
