What's included

Response to reported repairs, coordination with vetted local contractors, planned maintenance scheduling around inspections, and a record kept against the property so nothing gets forgotten between tenancies.

Why it matters

A property that's kept on top of stays lettable, holds its condition for a future sale, and gives tenants fewer reasons to complain - or leave. It's also central to staying compliant on gas, electrical and general safety standards.

Who this is for

Landlords already using our management service, and owners of tenanted or vacant property who want reliable, coordinated maintenance without managing contractors themselves.
