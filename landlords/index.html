Should I manage my rental property myself?

Some landlords manage very successfully on their own, particularly with one property nearby and the time to handle inspections, maintenance calls and compliance deadlines personally. It becomes harder with distance, multiple properties, or a difficult tenancy - and the compliance side (gas safety, EICR, deposit protection, right-to-rent) carries real penalties for landlords who fall behind. If you're weighing it up, our property management page sets out exactly what we take off your plate.
