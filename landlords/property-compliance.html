Landlord Compliance in Stoke-on-Trent

A plain-English overview of the main compliance requirements for landlords letting property in England. This is general information, not legal advice — rules change, so always check current government guidance.

What's generally required

  • Gas Safety Certificate (CP12) — annual, where the property has gas appliances
  • Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) — currently required every 5 years
  • Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) — required before marketing a property to let
  • Working smoke alarms on every floor and carbon monoxide alarms where required
  • Tenancy deposit protected in a government-approved scheme within 30 days
  • Right-to-rent checks on all tenants

We track renewal dates for every property we manage, so certificates don't lapse without you noticing. See our property management service for the full detail.

What landlord compliance checks are required?

Landlords letting property in England are required to hold a valid gas safety certificate where applicable, an Electrical Installation Condition Report, a valid EPC, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and to protect any deposit taken in a government-approved scheme. Requirements can change, so check current government guidance or speak to a managing agent.
