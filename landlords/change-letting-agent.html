Changing letting agent in Stoke-on-Trent

You can move a tenanted property to a new agent without disturbing the tenancy. Here's how it works with us.

  1. Check your current agreementLook for the notice period (often one to three months) and any fee for continuing to manage a tenant they introduced.
  2. Give notice in writingSend it to your current agent and keep a copy.
  3. Instruct usWe confirm the handover date and what we need from you.
  4. We collect the recordsTenancy agreement, referencing, inventory, deposit scheme details, and gas, electrical and EPC certificates with their renewal dates.
  5. Your tenant hears from usWe confirm the new rent payment details and how to report repairs, including our out-of-hours emergency line.

If the deposit is held by your current agent, it needs to be transferred or re-protected correctly. We'll check this with you as part of the handover.

Why landlords switch to us

Usually because they couldn't get hold of their agent, found out about repairs late, or waited too long for rent. With us, rent reaches you around five working days after the tenant pays, you get a statement each time and an invoice for every job, and repairs above your agreed limit need your approval. Full Management is 12% of the rent including VAT, with a one-off £180 set-up fee including VAT. See our fees.
