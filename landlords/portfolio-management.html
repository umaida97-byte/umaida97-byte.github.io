How we handle a portfolio

  • The same standard on every property: two inspections a year, compliance dates tracked, repairs within the limit you set
  • A statement with every payment and an invoice for every job, so your accounts are straightforward
  • Our network of tenants helps fill vacancies quickly as properties come free
  • Maintenance and refurbishment when a property needs work between tenancies
  • Sourcing and investment advice if you're adding to the portfolio

HMOs

Houses in multiple occupation carry extra rules on licensing, fire safety and room sizes, and more tenants to manage. We manage HMOs as part of Full Management. Licensing requirements are set by the council, so check Stoke-on-Trent City Council for the current position for your property, or ask us.
