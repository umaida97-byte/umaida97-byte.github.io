Why an accurate rent matters

Since 1 May 2026, rental properties in England must be advertised at a stated rent and landlords can't accept offers above it, so the advertised figure is the rent you'll get. Pitch it too high and the property sits empty; too low and you lose income for as long as the tenancy runs, because rent can now only be increased once a year. Frequently asked questions

How much does a rental valuation cost?

Nothing. Our rental valuations are free and there's no obligation to use our services afterwards.

How do you work out the rent?

We compare your property with similar homes we and others have let nearby, then adjust for its size, condition, parking, outside space and what tenants in that area are looking for. The street matters more than the town.

How accurate is an online rent estimate?

Online tools use averages across a wide area, so they can miss differences between streets and the condition of your property. A local valuation that takes those into account is usually more reliable.

Do you value properties in Newcastle-under-Lyme?

What happens after the valuation?
