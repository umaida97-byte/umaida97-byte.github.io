Best Areas of Stoke-on-Trent to Invest In

There's no single "best" area to invest in Stoke-on-Trent. The right one depends on your strategy. The best place to begin is our area guides: each one explains the housing, who rents there and what landlords should watch out for.

Explore the Area GuidesDiscuss Your Investment

Why there's no single answer

Stoke-on-Trent is six towns rather than one, and each has its own housing, tenants and prices. An area with strong student demand suits an HMO strategy but may be a poor fit for a family let, and the reverse is just as true. Yield, capital growth and how much management a property needs all change from town to town, and often from street to street.

Match the area to your strategy

  • City-centre apartments with higher turnover: Hanley, close to the shops, the Cultural Quarter and Staffordshire University.
  • Income-focused terraced houses: Burslem, Longton and Tunstall, where low purchase prices support stronger gross yields.
  • Long-term family lets: Tunstall, Fenton, Meir in Longton, and suburbs of Newcastle-under-Lyme such as Westlands and Clayton.
  • Professional tenants: Stoke, near the Royal Stoke University Hospital and the mainline railway station.
  • Student lets and HMOs: around Staffordshire University in Stoke and Hanley, and near Keele University in Newcastle-under-Lyme. See our HMO investment guide.
