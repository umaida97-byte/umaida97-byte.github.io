The process, step by step

  1. Valuation - an accurate, evidenced figure based on recent local sales.
  2. Instruction & preparation - professional photography, floorplans, and an EPC if you don't already have a valid one.
  3. Marketing - listed across the major property portals with proper description and imagery.
  4. Viewings & offers - accompanied viewings, with offers negotiated on your behalf.
  5. Sale agreed - solicitors instructed, and the conveyancing process begins.
  6. Sales progression - we chase surveys, searches and enquiries proactively rather than waiting on solicitors to call.
  7. Completion - funds transfer and keys are released.

Typical timescales

From instruction to completion commonly takes several months in the current market, though this varies significantly with chain length and mortgage processing times on both sides. A cash buyer with no chain can complete considerably faster.

How do I sell my property in Stoke-on-Trent?

Get a valuation, instruct an estate agent, have professional photography and marketing prepared, accept an offer, then proceed through conveyancing to completion. A proactive agent keeps the legal process moving rather than leaving it to solicitors alone.
