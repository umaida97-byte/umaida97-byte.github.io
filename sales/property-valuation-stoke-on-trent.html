What determines your property's value

  • Recent comparable sales on your street or in your immediate area
  • Condition and any recent updates
  • Property type, size and layout
  • Current buyer demand for that property type locally
  • Broader market conditions

Why a local, in-person valuation matters

Automated online valuations are a reasonable starting point, but they can't account for the specific condition of your kitchen, a recent extension, or the fact that three similar houses on your street sold within the last six months at prices the algorithm hasn't caught up with yet. A valuation from someone who actively sells in your area will generally be more accurate.
