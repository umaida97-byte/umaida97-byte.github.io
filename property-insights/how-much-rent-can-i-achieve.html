How Much Rent Can I Achieve for My Property?

Achievable rent depends on comparable lettings nearby, condition, and current tenant demand — not a fixed formula.

What determines achievable rent

The clearest guide is what similar properties on the same or nearby streets have actually let for recently, adjusted for condition, size and any features like parking or a garden. Broader area averages are a starting point at best.

Why condition changes the number

A well-presented property with an updated kitchen or bathroom will often achieve meaningfully more than an identical but tired property nearby — sometimes enough to justify the cost of the work. See our guide to preparing a property for letting.

Getting an accurate figure

The only reliable way to know is a rental valuation from someone actively letting property in your specific area right now — we offer this free and with no obligation.
