Selling Property

Should I Sell or Rent My Property?

A common question with no universal answer - it comes down to your finances, plans and appetite for being a landlord.

Reasons to sell

You need the equity for another purchase, you don't want the ongoing responsibility of being a landlord, or the property doesn't suit letting well in its current condition or location.

Reasons to rent it out

You can afford to hold it, local rental demand is strong, and you're comfortable either managing a tenancy yourself or paying an agent to do it. Renting also keeps the option to sell later, once you've had time to decide.
