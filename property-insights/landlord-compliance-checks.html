What Landlord Compliance Checks Are Required?

A plain-English overview — general information, not legal advice, since rules do change.

The core requirements

Gas Safety Certificate (annual, where applicable), an Electrical Installation Condition Report (currently every 5 years), a valid EPC, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, deposit protection within 30 days, and right-to-rent checks on all tenants.

Why this matters beyond box-ticking

Missing a renewal isn't just a compliance technicality — it can affect a landlord's ability to serve notice, and carries potential fines. See our full compliance guide for more detail.

Frequently asked questions

What landlord compliance checks are required?

Landlords letting property in England are required to hold a valid gas safety certificate where applicable, an Electrical Installation Condition Report, a valid EPC, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and to protect any deposit taken in a government-approved scheme. Requirements can change, so check current government guidance or speak to a managing agent.
