Selling Property

How Much Is My House Worth?

It depends on condition, location and what's genuinely selling nearby right now.

What determines value

Recent comparable sales on your street, condition, property type and size, current buyer demand, and broader market conditions all combine to set a realistic figure.

Why online valuations only go so far

Automated tools are a reasonable starting point but can't account for your specific kitchen, a recent extension, or sales on your street the algorithm hasn't caught up with yet. See our full valuation guide, or book a free valuation directly.

Frequently asked questions

How much is my house worth in Stoke-on-Trent?

Your property's value depends on its condition, exact location, comparable recent sales nearby, and current buyer demand for that property type. The only reliable way to get an accurate figure is a valuation from someone who knows recent sales on your specific street - we offer a free, no-obligation valuation.
