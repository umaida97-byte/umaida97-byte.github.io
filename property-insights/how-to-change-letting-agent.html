Landlord Advice

How to Change Letting Agent

More common than landlords expect, and more straightforward than it sounds.

Check your notice period

Most management agreements require written notice, commonly one to three months - check your existing contract before doing anything else.

Give notice, then instruct your new agent

Once notice is given in writing, your new agent should handle the transfer of tenancy records, deposit registration details and compliance certificate renewal dates on your behalf. See our full switching guide for the complete process.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change letting agent?

Check the notice period in your current management agreement, give written notice, then instruct your new agent to handle the transfer of tenancy records, deposit registration details and compliance certificate dates.
