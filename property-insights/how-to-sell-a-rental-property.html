Selling Property

How to Sell a Rental Property

Selling with a tenant in place involves a few extra considerations beyond a standard sale.

Selling with a tenant in situ

Some buyers - particularly other investors - are happy to buy with a tenant already in place, which can widen your buyer pool if the tenancy is well documented and rent is up to date.

Selling with vacant possession

Other buyers, especially owner-occupiers, will only proceed once the property is empty, which means serving appropriate notice and allowing time for the tenant to leave before marketing or completion.

Getting the timing right

The right approach depends on your tenant's situation and your own timeline - we can talk through both options and what each means for likely buyer pool and timescale.
