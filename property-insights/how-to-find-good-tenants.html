How to Find Good Tenants

Good tenants aren't luck — they're mostly the result of proper referencing and a well-presented property.

Referencing properly

Credit checks, employment and income verification, and references from a current or previous landlord are the core of proper referencing. Skipping steps to fill a vacancy faster is usually a false economy.

Right-to-rent checks

A legal requirement in England — every tenant's right to rent in the UK must be verified before a tenancy begins.

A property that attracts the right applicants

A well-presented, accurately priced property tends to attract a stronger pool of applicants in the first place, which makes good referencing decisions easier.
