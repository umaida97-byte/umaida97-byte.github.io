Property Management

What Does a Property Management Company Actually Do?

A practical breakdown of what's actually involved, beyond the marketing language.

The core work

Finding and referencing tenants, collecting rent and chasing arrears, arranging and coordinating maintenance, carrying out inspections, and tracking compliance certificates and their renewal dates.

The less visible work

Handling tenant queries and disputes, managing renewals and rent reviews, coordinating check-outs and deposit resolution, and keeping records that would stand up to scrutiny if a dispute or claim arose.

Why it adds up to more than it sounds

Individually, each task is manageable. Across a full tenancy — or a portfolio of them — the combined admin and compliance tracking is where a management company earns its fee. See our full property management service.
