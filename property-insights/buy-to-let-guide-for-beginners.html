Buy-to-Let Guide for Beginners

The essentials for anyone considering their first rental property purchase.

Financing

Buy-to-let mortgages typically require a larger deposit than residential lending — commonly 25% or more — and lend against expected rental income rather than salary alone.

Choosing a property

First-time investors often do best with a straightforward single-let in an area with clear tenant demand, rather than a more complex HMO strategy. Condition matters as much as price.

Ongoing responsibilities

Once you own the property, referencing, compliance and either self-management or instructing an agent all follow. See our full buy-to-let guide for more detail.
