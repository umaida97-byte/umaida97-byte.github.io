How to Calculate Rental Yield

The formula is simple; getting an accurate, honest figure is where it matters.

Gross yield

Gross yield = (annual rent ÷ property value) × 100. Example (illustrative only): a £120,000 property achieving £750 pcm (£9,000/year) has a gross yield of 7.5%.

Net yield

Net yield deducts annual running costs — management fees, maintenance, insurance and an allowance for void periods — before dividing by property value, giving a more realistic return figure. See our full rental yields guide.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate rental yield?

Gross rental yield is calculated as annual rental income divided by the property's purchase price (or current value), multiplied by 100. Net yield takes the same calculation but deducts annual costs such as management fees, maintenance, insurance and void periods before dividing by the property value.
