How Much Does Property Management Cost?

Fees vary by agency and by service level — here's what typically drives the cost.

What affects the fee

Management fees are usually charged as a percentage of monthly rent, and vary based on the level of service (tenant find only, versus rent collection, versus full management), the number of properties, and the local market.

What you're paying for

At the full management level, the fee covers referencing, rent collection, inspections, maintenance coordination and compliance tracking — work that carries real time cost and, if missed, real financial and legal risk for a landlord.

Getting a specific figure
