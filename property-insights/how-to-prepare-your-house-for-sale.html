Selling Property

How to Prepare Your House for Sale

Small, inexpensive changes tend to matter more than people expect.

Presentation basics

A thorough declutter and clean, minor repairs attended to, and neutral decoration where rooms are strongly personalised all help buyers picture themselves in the space.

Kerb appeal

The first impression from the street affects how buyers approach the rest of the viewing - tidy the front garden, driveway and entrance before anything else.

Paperwork

Have your EPC ready, and gather any warranties or planning permissions for extensions or major work - buyers' solicitors will ask for these during conveyancing, and having them ready avoids delay.
